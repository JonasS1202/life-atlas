# Areas of life

Each area is a folder with a `README.md` (what belongs here, overview, links to its pages). Atlas creates areas when the
user's life needs them, names them in {{LANGUAGE}}, and lists them here and in the map of `INDEX.md`.

Typical areas (create only what is real for this user): finances, insurance, health, home, mobility, work, education,
family, hobbies, digital (accounts, devices, subscriptions), legal and documents.

A thing lives in exactly one area. A car insurance policy lives in insurance and is linked from mobility, not copied.
